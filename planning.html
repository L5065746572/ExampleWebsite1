Planning Documentation

Introduction

Explain the purpose of this section and why you have created it

Project Management

In this section, explain how you will manage your project. At minimum, include a gantt chart with explanations. You may then add additional tools that demonstrate project planning skills, including MOSCOW, SWOT, feasibility study, scope statements. You may also explain how software developer lifecycle theories can be applied to your project.

Design Documentation

In this section, present and annotate design documentation for your project. Design tools to be used may include Mind Maps, Mood boards, Drawings/Sketches, Flow charts/wireframes, Pseudo coding,, Venn Diagrams, Audio Cue Sheet, Storyboards, or others. Successful design documentation will show clearly what you intend to create and explain the reasons for your choices,

Peer Review

Have your designs reviewed by others to test suitability, using interviews, forms, email, or other suitable methods. Consider if the designs are clear and understandable by others. Why are you gathering feedback? Do your ideas have the intended effect? Are there changes you need to make to your ideas?

Alternative/discarded ideas

Document at minimum one alternative design idea and explain why it wasn’t chosen.

Evaluation - Design

How successful was your design documentation? Do your designs clearly explain what you intend to create? Could they be followed by another professional? Did you need to make any changes to your designs?
