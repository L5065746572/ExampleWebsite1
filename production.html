Testing & Peer Reviews

You should gather supporting evidence for your evaluation, include systematic testing or gathering feedback from others. Other tools may also be used to gather objective evidence that supports your evaluation. Explain how you’ve gathered this evidence and what it means.

Evaluation - Practical

Evaluate your practical work. Did you meet your objectives in your project proposal? What went well in your practical? What could be improved? Suggest three improvements you could make to your project (which may be detailed with additional designs or evidence of how you’ve carried these improvements out). Support your evaluations with evidence such as testing and peer reviews.
