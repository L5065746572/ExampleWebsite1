Planning Documentation

Introduction

Carry out a risk assessment on your project to anticipate and plan for problems encountered. Attached is a standard cybersecurity risk assessment template however other types of risk assessments may also be appropriate. In addition, you must log and describe how you’ve solved problems encountered in your reflective diary

Risk Assessmnet

Identify a minimum of two cybersecurity threats that are relevant to your project. Describe what the threat is and explain how it works. Use the risk matrix above to get an overall risk severity. Then suggest actions that can be taken to counter the threat.
